# Implementation plan

## Open tasks

### RL-019 — [FEAT] Hold weapon rigs with fixed grips on one or more bodies

- Dependency: None.
- Why: The owner wants the chibi to hold weapon rigs: files with a fixed model and fixed grip points that replace today's ad hoc prop. That prop takes its grip spacing from wherever the hands happen to be (`toggleGrip` in `src/solve/holds.ts`). A key holds at most one prop with one or two grips (`src/format/constraints.ts`), but dual daggers need two independent rigid bodies, so the key format has to grow.
- Scope: Add a closed `riglab.weapon` version 1 format in `src/format/weapon.ts`. A weapon has a `name` and one or more `bodies`. Each body has a `name`, an art `slot`, an `axis` (butt and tip in the body's own frame, for its line and turn handle) and one or two `grips`. Each grip has a `name`, the hand `chain` it binds and its fixed point `at` in the body's frame. A chain grips at most once per weapon. A body with two grips is free: the key places it (`x`, `y`, `rotation`) and both hands solve to its grips. A body with one grip rides on that hand: the grip also fixes the body's `angle` to the lower bone, so the body follows the hand and nothing else places it. `riglab.document` version 1 gains an optional `weapons` list with a copy of each weapon rig its keys hold. Each copy is validated against the rig (every grip names one of its chains), so documents stay self-contained. A key gains an optional `weapon`: the weapon's `name`, and `bodies` placing each of its free bodies by name. A key holds a `prop` or a `weapon`, never both, and a gripped chain cannot also be pinned. `holdConstraints` solves every grip of every free body with the existing two-bone IK: rotation only, inside angle limits and on the chain's bend side. It then places the riding bodies on the solved hands. A grip a hand cannot reach is reported with its weapon, body, grip and shortfall, and the limb stays at its closest reach. A free body moves between keys that hold the same weapon, eased like the pose, and otherwise holds still, the same as pins. Engine playback (`engineOf`, `playAt`) carries weapons the way it carries the prop. Documents with the old `prop` validate, play, bake and export exactly as before, so nothing needs migrating.
- Non-goals: Weapon files and art (RL-020), editor UI (RL-021), export bundles and bakes (RL-022), migrating old props, one hand on a two-grip body, and weapons for rigs whose hands are not the chibi's chains.
- Acceptance: The weapon validator refuses each of the following with its JSON path: unknown fields, duplicate bodies or grips, a chain bound twice, more than two grips on a body, an `angle` on a two-grip body or none on a one-grip body, and non-finite points. A document refuses a key whose weapon is missing from `weapons`, whose placements do not match the weapon's free bodies, or that holds both a prop and a weapon. Every existing document test, including the prop ones, passes unchanged. Holding a two-grip test weapon at a reachable placement puts both hands on their grips to 1e-6, and every bone keeps its rest length. Moving it out of reach reports the shortfall and changes no length. Two one-grip bodies follow their hands at their grips' angles, and blending moves a free body between two keys. `npm run check` passes.
- Validation: Pinned `npm ci`, focused format and solve tests, `npm run check`, `npm run audit:dependencies`, `check:patch`, `git diff --check`, and exact-head CI.
- Authorities: `src/format/constraints.ts`, `src/format/document.ts`, `src/format/animation.ts`, `src/solve/constraints.ts`, `src/solve/holds.ts`, `src/solve/engine.ts`, `src/solve/ik.ts`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and owner direction on 2026-10-13.

### RL-020 — [FEAT] Add the sword, spear, axe and daggers weapon rigs and art

- Dependency: RL-019.
- Why: These are the first four weapons the owner asked for, at chibi scale with original art.
- Scope: Add `characters/weapons/<name>.weapon.json` for `sword`, `spear`, `axe` and `daggers`, with part SVGs in `characters/weapons/parts/`. The parts use the strict part format the chibi uses and the same parser reads them. `src/weapons/` loads the weapons, validates them against the chibi and parses their parts into a new `weapons` art set. The set's stylesheet palette reuses the chibi's outline, gold and wood shades plus a steel tone. Scale follows the chibi: 91 units tall, arms 10 + 11, mitten fists about 7.6 units across. The sword is one free body with two grips on its hilt and the blade pointing up. The spear is one free body with two grips on its shaft and the point toward +x, the facing direction. The axe is one free body with two grips on its haft and the head up. The daggers are two one-grip bodies, one per hand (`arm-front`, `arm-back`), in a reverse grip: the blade comes out of the pinky side of the fist and points down along the forearm when the hand is raised. Grip spacing fits two fists and stays reachable. Docs list the weapons and the art set.
- Non-goals: Drawing weapons in the editor (RL-021), more weapons, per-weapon animations, and copying any existing game's assets.
- Acceptance: Every weapon file validates against the chibi, and every part parses. A test holds each weapon in a normal two-hand pose, with both feet planted and the weapon in front of the body. For each free body, both hands reach their grips to 1e-6, and every bone stays at its rest length and inside its limits. In a raised guard, each dagger's blade points down (positive y) within 15° of the line from the fist to the elbow. `npm run check` passes.
- Validation: Pinned `npm ci`, focused weapon tests, `npm run check`, `npm run audit:dependencies`, `check:patch`, `git diff --check`, a rendered preview of the four weapons beside the chibi, and exact-head CI.
- Authorities: `characters/chibi/`, `src/chibi/chibi.ts`, `src/hexframe/parts.ts`, `src/app/styles.css`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and owner direction on 2026-10-13.

### RL-021 — [FEAT] Pick, pose and draw weapons in the editor

- Dependency: RL-019 and RL-020.
- Why: Holding a weapon has to be an editor action, and the canvas has to show and move what is held.
- Scope: In Pose mode the right panel offers a Weapon picker (None, Sword, Spear, Axe, Daggers) for the key at the current frame, with or without a selected bone. Picking a weapon binds the hands to its grips and places each free body so the midpoint of its grips sits at the midpoint of the hands, at the weapon's authored rotation. It also copies the weapon rig into the document's `weapons`, drops copies no key holds, re-solves, and reports in the status bar any grip out of reach. None lets go. A free body gets move and turn handles on the canvas, and dragging a hand that grips it carries it, as the prop does today. Riding bodies follow their hands. The weapon picker replaces Hold a prop and Grip prop in the pose inspector. An old document's prop still draws, moves and turns, and can be let go. The art layer draws weapon parts from the `weapons` set at each body's transform. Each body paints just under the topmost forearm that grips it, in Hexframe's paint order. With Art off, each body draws as a line along its axis. Undo restores the previous hold in one step. Docs describe it.
- Non-goals: Export and bake (RL-022), editing weapon rigs in the app, and depth profiles beyond facing right.
- Acceptance: Reducer tests pick each weapon on a key and undo it, refuse a pick between keys the same way a pose drop there is refused, keep the document valid, and drop unused weapon copies. A canvas-drag test moves and turns a free body with both hands following, and an out-of-reach move is reported without stretching. A browser pass shows the chibi holding each weapon with Art on and off, with no console errors. `npm run check` passes.
- Validation: Pinned `npm ci`, focused editor and viewer tests, `npm run check`, `npm run audit:dependencies`, `check:patch`, `git diff --check`, a headless Chrome pass on a worktree dev server, and exact-head CI.
- Authorities: `src/app/PoseInspector.tsx`, `src/app/useCanvasDrags.ts`, `src/viewer/RigCanvas.tsx`, `src/viewer/ArtLayer.tsx`, `src/hexframe/art.ts`, `src/editor/poseEdits.ts`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and owner direction on 2026-10-13.

### RL-022 — [FEAT] Export and bake weapon transforms, with an acceptance test per weapon

- Dependency: RL-021.
- Why: Consumers need the weapons along with the motion, and each weapon needs an end-to-end proof like the spear thrust.
- Scope: An export bundle writes each document weapon as `weapons/<name>.weapon.json` and lists it with its SHA-256 in an optional manifest `weapons` list. Import verifies those files like every other file and validates animations against the bundled weapons; bundles without weapons read as before. A baked file records each body's world `[x, y, rotation°]` by name, riding bodies included, on every frame that holds a weapon, next to the existing prop entries. The Hexframe clip export carries the held poses and reports that weapon transforms are left out, because Hexframe's clip has no channel for them. `tests/acceptance/weapons.test.ts` drives the editor's reducer once per weapon. The chibi holds the weapon with both feet pinned through an attack's four keys, then exports with baked frames, zips, unzips and reloads.
- Non-goals: Weapon support in Hexframe itself, formats beyond optional fields, and weapon physics.
- Acceptance: For each of the four weapons, the reloaded document equals the original and re-baking equals the exported bake. On every frame, every bone keeps its rest length and offset (to 1e-5), both feet stay on their pins, and each dagger stays on its hand along its forearm. Each free body's grips sit on their hands at the 13 pose frames to 1e-5, and within half a unit between them, where Hexframe interpolates joint angles rather than grips. Hexframe's sampler on the exported clip places every bone where RigLab baked it. Old bundles, and documents with props, still import and bake as before. `npm run check` passes.
- Validation: Pinned `npm ci`, focused export and acceptance tests, `npm run check`, `npm run audit:dependencies`, `check:patch`, `git diff --check`, a browser export and re-import, and exact-head CI.
- Authorities: `src/export/bundle.ts`, `src/export/bake.ts`, `src/hexframe/adapter.ts`, `tests/acceptance/spear-thrust.test.ts`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and owner direction on 2026-10-13.
