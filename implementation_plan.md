### RL-051 — [FIX] Bend the front arm's elbow outward

- Dependency: RL-050, because flipping the bend makes the solver pick angles a turn away from the stored ones.
- Why: The owner reported on 2026-10-16: "forearm front is angling the wrong way it should joint the other direction". Both arm chains in the chibi rig bend −1, so the front arm (`arm-front`, on the +x side) folds its elbow inward across the chest. The frame audit finds `forearm-front` between −102° and −128° in every frame of the sword, spear and axe animations, while the back arm folds outward. A prototype with the front chain bent +1 shows both elbows out and both fists closing on the grip.
- Scope: Set the `arm-front` chain's `bend` to 1 in `placeholder-library/rigs/chibi.rig.json`. Right after the merge, change the owner's `~/Documents/RigLab/rigs/chibi.rig.json` the same way, after copying it to `backups/<UTC stamp>-rl-051/`. Keys keep their stored rotations. Held grips are solved with the new bend as they play, and unarmed and dagger keys are forward kinematics that the bend does not touch. Update the chibi test and ARCHITECTURE (The chibi).
- Non-goals: Joint limits, rewriting stored keys, mirroring the unarmed motion (the prototype showed both hands meeting at the centre in the walk), and the back arm.
- Acceptance: The chibi test pins both arm chains' bends. A solver test holds the sword at rest and finds `forearm-front` folded outward (a positive local rotation). Headless Chrome on a worktree dev server over a copy of the owner's folder shows both elbows out in the sword, spear and axe animations, in the editor and the wardrobe. `check:library` passes on the owner's folder after the change, and the backup holds the previous rig. `npm run check` passes.
- Validation: Pinned `npm ci`, focused chibi and solver tests, `npm run check`, `npm run audit:dependencies`, `check:patch`, `git diff --check`, the headless pass, `npm run check:library` on the owner's folder, and exact-head CI.

### RL-052 — [FIX] Draw held weapons over the head with the hands over their grips

- Dependency: RL-051, so the hands close on the grips from outside.
- Why: The owner reported on 2026-10-16: "props should show on top of the head. swords are clipping weird". A weapon body paints just under its topmost gripping forearm, and the head paints after every arm, so the head covers a weapon raised near it. The inspection found this in two places. The blue-mage skin's hair hides the sword blade and the axe head at rest, and a dagger slides behind the head in frames 22–27 of the daggers jump. Because the sword sits above `forearm-front`, its handle also covers the front fist, so only one hand seems to hold it. In the unarmed and daggers jumps, the airborne key raises both arms straight up behind the head, so the hands vanish. In every jump the crouch drops the pelvis faster than the legs fold, and the ankles sink up to 1.2 units below the ground in frames 3–9.
- Scope: `withWeaponArt` paints every held weapon body after the whole figure, the head included, and then the forearms that grip it, so the fists close over the grip. The editor, the wardrobe and baked art share the rule. In the owner's folder, after a backup, re-pose the jump. The airborne key raises the unarmed and dagger hands up and out beside the head instead of behind it. Every class's jump keeps both ankles on the ground through the crouch. Update the weapon-art tests and ARCHITECTURE (Art paint order).
- Non-goals: Depth-profile changes, foot bones, art changes, and other motion. The shoes still tilt with the shins, because the chibi has no foot bones; this is reported, not changed.
- Acceptance: Art-order tests cover two cases. With the sword, spear or axe, `head` paints before the weapon and the weapon before both gripping forearms. Each dagger paints after the head and before the forearm that holds it. Headless Chrome on a worktree dev server over a copy of the owner's folder shows the weapon over the head and hair for every class and skin, both fists on two-handed grips, and no fist over the face in the jumps. The frame audit finds no ankle below the ground and no weapon under the head. `check:library` passes on the owner's folder after the re-pose, and the backup holds the previous files. `npm run check` passes.
- Validation: Pinned `npm ci`, focused art and weapon tests, `npm run check`, `npm run audit:dependencies`, `check:patch`, `git diff --check`, the headless pass, the frame audit, `npm run check:library` on the owner's folder, and exact-head CI.
